# CivicPulse visual direction

## World

CivicPulse should feel like a trusted neighborhood publication: a place where local observations become a visible, well-kept public record. The visual language combines field-guide clarity with editorial confidence. The interactive map is the hero's signature media; the page around it stays calm and legible.

## Palette and type

- Warm chalk canvas: `#f7f6f0`, with quiet limestone surfaces.
- Deep forest ink: `#172d25`, used for the hero, primary actions, and core text.
- Fresh chartreuse: `#d5ed7b`, reserved for the main action and map emphasis.
- Muted terracotta: used sparingly for attention pins and destructive/error states.
- Epilogue/Avenir-style geometric sans with measured, tight display headlines and open body copy.

## Composition

- A dark, spacious map-led masthead gives the product one strong first impression.
- Issue discovery is a two-column editorial index on wide screens and a single-column reading list on phones.
- Issue detail uses one calm reading surface, a plain progress timeline, and source status that remains explicit.
- Campaigns are a separate light sage-green area. Pledge intent and the public supporter ledger are visually legible without implying payments have happened.
- Controls use restrained radii and visible keyboard focus. Borders and soft shadows are used only to distinguish interactive or raised surfaces.

## Product truth

The Bhubaneswar map and reports are illustrative samples. Supporter display names are self-reported. A pledge intent is not money received, and an API-backed campaign is public only when the API is available. No report is represented as delivered to a civic office without a confirmed integration.
